Wedding Photos

Our day, through everyone’s eyes.

Wedding Photos is a personal photo and video album for Raquel and Aaron’s wedding. It gives our guests a place to share the happy tears, the dance floor, and all the little moments in between.

One album for all of us

Open the wedding invitation link or enter the album code to upload photos and videos, browse everyone’s completed uploads, and download original files. Guests do not need a Google account. You can add your name and a note, or leave both blank.

Anyone with the guest invitation link or album code can view and download the shared photos, videos, names, and notes. Please share moments you are comfortable including in the wedding album.

Saved with our memories

The album owner connects Google Drive to keep the original files in a dedicated wedding folder. Only the owner signs in with Google. The app uses the drive.file permission to work with files it creates or that the owner specifically selects for it; it does not request access to the owner’s entire Drive.
