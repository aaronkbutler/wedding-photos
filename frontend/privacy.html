How our personal wedding album handles your information.

Last updated October 7, 2026

What you share

Wedding Photos collects the photos and videos you choose to upload, their file names and technical details, and any optional name or caption you provide. Original files can contain information added by your camera, such as the date or location; the app does not remove that embedded information.

We use this information to receive uploads, organize the shared album, display photos and videos, and let guests download the originals. Guests do not need a Google account.

Who can see the album

Anyone with the guest invitation link or album code can browse and download completed, visible uploads, including their names and captions. This access can be passed along if someone shares the link or code. The album owner can also access uploaded files directly in Google Drive.

We do not sell album information or use it for advertising.

The owner’s Google connection

Only the album owner connects a Google account. We request openid and email to verify the owner’s identity, and drive.file to create and manage the wedding folder and its files. That Drive permission is limited to files the app creates or the owner specifically selects for it.

We use the owner’s Google account information and authorization only to provide and manage the album. The owner’s verified email and offline authorization are stored in Google Secret Manager. Google credentials are never given to guests. The owner can revoke the app’s connection through their Google account permissions; doing so prevents further Drive access but does not delete existing files.

Where information is kept

Original photos and videos are stored in the owner’s Google Drive. Album metadata, including names, captions, file details, and upload status, is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Google Cloud Run receives uploads and delivers album media to guests. GitHub Pages hosts the album’s web pages.

The album uses your browser’s session storage to remember the album code in that tab, when the browser permits it. The owner dashboard uses a sign-in cookie. Hosting providers may also process technical request information, such as IP addresses, as part of operating their services.

Keeping or removing a moment

The album is intended as a wedding keepsake and has no automatic deletion deadline. The owner can hide an upload from the shared album; hiding it keeps the original in Drive. Copies already downloaded by guests cannot be withdrawn through this app.

To ask about your information or request that a photo, video, name, or caption be removed, contact Raquel or Aaron using the contact details on our wedding site or your invitation. Please identify the item so we can find it and explain whether you want it hidden from the album or removed from our stored collection.
